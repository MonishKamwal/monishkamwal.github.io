Sketches · About — with a signature, on warm paper

Not bland this time

You liked the warm-paper ground but the nine layouts were too plain next to the Home scroll and the inner pages — each of those has a signature device (the isometric architecture deck, the drawn journey serpentine, the skills blob-wash) and a bolder editorial hand. These four give About its own signature, and keep the warm paper throughout. Same content as before.

Same in every sketch: name + "the person behind the machine", the two-paragraph bio, Work (3) + Education with the résumé link, and "Say hello" + email + GitHub / LinkedIn. Try it: Desktop / Phone toggle up top.

B1

Editorial profile

The device is voice. A magazine opener: the name set enormous in Bodoni (surname in ochre italic), the portrait crashing into the headline, a drop-cap lead, and the CV dropped to two quiet monospaced ledgers underneath. No illustration — just confident typography and composition, the way a good profile page carries itself.

B2

Career trace

Borrows the Journey page's drawn serpentine and turns it on a life: the Work and Education history is one ochre line winding down the page, each role a node with its card hanging off the alternating side. About and Journey end up speaking the same visual language. (The line can draw itself in on arrival, like Journey's does.)

B3

Behind the machine

Takes the tagline literally. A faint blueprint of the pipeline — Data → Train → Ship → Serve → Monitor — is drawn across the whole page, and the person stands in front of it, the bio and CV lifted on a subtle paper panel. Ties straight back to the Architecture page; the most conceptual of the four.

B4

Portrait in the wash

Brings the Skills blob-wash to About — soft warm shapes (ochre, sand, rose) multiplied on the paper, with the portrait resting among them and the story set calm alongside. Warmest and most organic; makes About feel like a sibling of the Skills screen.
