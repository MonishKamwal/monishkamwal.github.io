Sketches · About page — nine grounds, no blue

Who are you, without the blue

The current About page sits on a dusty-blue wash. Every sketch below drops it. They keep the same content — the bio, the Work and Education timeline, and "Say hello" — and each tries a different ground, layout and way of setting the CV. Pick one (ground and layout can be mixed) and I'll build it into the real About page.

Same in every sketch: the portrait, the two-paragraph bio (still placeholder), Work (3 roles) + Education with the résumé link, and the click-to-copy email + GitHub / LinkedIn. Try it: toggle Desktop / Phone up top — each design reflows.

A1

Warm paper

The smallest change: the blue swapped for a warm ivory, everything else kept close to the current page — so you can judge the palette in isolation before changing anything structural. Portrait card, vertical ink timeline, ochre links.

A2

Editorial white

No wash at all. An oversized Bodoni "About" over a rule, a clean two-column feel, and the CV set as a ruled ledger of dated rows. Reads like a well-set page in a book — the most restrained, most "print" option.

A3

Sage study

Borrows Architecture's sage so About still belongs to the site's family of grounds — just green instead of blue. A round portrait softens it; the timeline keeps a faint line with ochre dots. Calm and natural.

A4

Blush

Borrows Skills' rose, softened. The warmest, most personal of the on-palette grounds — fitting for the one page that's actually about a person rather than a system.

A5

Split panel

A magazine split: a solid colour panel on the left carries the portrait, the name and "Say hello"; the open paper side carries the bio and CV. The most editorial, most "cover + spread" layout. (Panel colour is a placeholder deep ochre — easy to retune.)

A6

Charcoal

Lights off. A near-black ground with warm ochre accents and the portrait in a lit frame — "the person behind the machine" read as a late-night workbench. The boldest departure; dramatic, and a strong contrast to the bright Home scroll.

A7

Warm gradient

A soft cream-to-blush gradient rather than a flat wash — subtle motion in the ground itself — with the Work history stepping across as a horizontal timeline instead of running down. Modern and warm. (Collapses back to a vertical list on phone.)

A8

Ledger

The CV as a real document: monospaced dates, ruled rows, section labels set in small caps. A technical register that suits someone who builds systems — the résumé is the design. Warm off-white keeps it from feeling cold.

A9

Minimal centred

Maximum air. Everything centred on a warm off-white, a small circular portrait up top, the timeline reduced to a quiet centred list. Lets the words carry it — the calmest, most confident option.

Nine grounds, all blue-free. The ground, the layout and the CV treatment are independent — e.g. "warm paper ground with A8's ledger CV", or "A5's split with a charcoal panel" — so tell me a combination, not just a number, if you'd like to mix. Then I'll wire the chosen look into the real About page in sections-prototype.html.
