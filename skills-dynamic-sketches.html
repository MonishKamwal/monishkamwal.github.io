Sketches · Home §5 "Skills" — the same shapes, now in motion

Ways to make the map move

You liked the composition — five competencies as overlapping shapes, with a tool each pair shares written once out in the crossing. What's missing is life: it sits still until you hover it. Every sketch keeps the exact composition and the four measured crossings, and adds a different kind of motion. S1–S5 are the first pass; S6–S9 are the follow-up — the Living wash you liked, made unmistakable. Pick one and I'll build it into transition-prototype.html.

Unchanged in every sketch: the five shapes, their colours, the four crossings (S3, ONNX, k6, GitHub Actions), the "See all skills →" way-out, and the top-left / bottom-centre kept clear for the head and the ↓ cue. Try it: hover any shape in any sketch — the live hover still works and takes over from the animation.

S1

Living wash

The lightest touch. Every shape drifts, breathes and rotates a hair on its own slow clock, so the whole field is gently alive — like paper shapes floating in water. As two shapes drift together their overlap deepens on its own (they multiply), and the tool in that crossing fades up exactly when the overlap is deepest. Nothing to click; it just never holds still.

S2

Auto-spotlight

The direct cousin of §3's architecture deck. The field rests faded and a spotlight visits one competency at a time on a timer — it comes to full colour, its name turns ochre and its shared tools lift, holds a beat, then hands to the next. Dots and arrows let you steer it; hover takes over, same as the deck. Reads as "here is what I do, one thing at a time."

S3

Metaball — the shapes fuse

The most physical. The shapes become true liquid blobs: as they drift toward each other they stretch, bridge and merge through a gooey filter, then pull apart again. The shared tool blooms in only while its two blobs are actually fused. Boldest of the five — it stops being a diagram and becomes a lava-lamp of competencies.

S4

Assembles on arrival

The motion is an entrance, not a loop. As the section comes into view each shape grows out of its own centre in sequence, and the instant a pair of shapes meets, its shared tool snaps in with a little pop — so you watch the crossings get built. It settles and holds. A quiet "↺ replay" lets you watch it assemble again.

S5

Pipeline tour

Motion with a story. A travelling marker walks the real MLOps loop — Data → Training → Shipping → Watching → Front — lighting each shape as it arrives and tracing the shared tool on the way (S3 from data into training, ONNX into shipping, and so on). It reads as the pipeline flowing through the competencies, then loops. The most "it all connects" of the five.

Follow-up · the Living wash, louder

More life in the wash

You liked S1 but it was almost too quiet to be sure it was running. These four stay in the same family — ambient, always-on, nothing to click — but each makes the motion unmistakable a different way. S6 is literally S1 with the dial turned up; S7–S9 change what moves.

S6

Living wash — turned up

The exact S1 engine, louder: shapes drift twice as far, lean and breathe more, and the colour pulses on its own faster clock so a shape visibly comes forward and recedes — its name warming toward ochre as it does. Same idea you liked, now you can't miss it. The most "safe" step up from S1.

S7

Morphing blobs

Here the shapes barely travel — instead their outlines undulate and each blob swells and shrinks, a slow ripple running around it like a jellyfish or a lava lamp. Because the centres hold, the four crossings stay put while the overlaps deepen and ease as the shapes breathe. Reads as the most liquid of the family, and never drifts the composition out of balance.

S8

The current

The shapes sway together, on a slow tide whose direction itself drifts — but the front shapes swing further than the ones behind, so the overlaps genuinely open and close as the field leans. Coordinated motion catches the eye more than independent jitter: it reads as one living thing breathing, not five.

S9

Colour wave

The shapes hold near-still; what moves is a soft band of full colour sweeping across the field left to right, lifting each competency — and the crossing it reaches — to full strength in turn, then letting it settle back. The thing you notice is the hue travelling, which the eye catches even at a glance. Closest to the site's quiet, editorial feel while being clearly alive.
